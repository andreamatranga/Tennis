Drag the blue dot (you). Color = fastest ball arrival in seconds. The visible area includes the doubles court, side tramlines, and back/side outfields. In bounces mode, hover any cell to see that shot's trajectory and its 45° shadow. Enable the opponent to switch to a difference map.
Left-drag the coral dot to move the opponent; right-drag to aim his velocity (direction and speed). A point is a winner (green) only if some legal ball reaches it that he cannot intercept within his reach before the ball's second bounce or it leaves the court; otherwise it's red (his). Hover any cell to preview his best intercept run; left-click a cell to play the exchange in real time.

Model: drag-free parabolic flight, net-height interpolated across the width (lower at centre, higher at the posts), first bounce must land in the singles court (or service box in serve mode), with restitution-damped bounces after. Every speed from ~3 m/s up to the ceiling and a full fan of aim targets are swept; each cell keeps the single fastest arrival, air or post-bounce. The opponent's reach uses a reaction delay, finite acceleration, capped run speed, and his initial momentum. Spin, air drag, and 3D racket geometry are not modeled.
